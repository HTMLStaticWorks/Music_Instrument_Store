MISSION

Crafting Sound & Connecting Communities

Allegro Music Store was founded in 1995 with a simple mission: to make high-quality musical instruments accessible to our community. We believe that music is a universal language that enriches lives and strengthens connections.

What started as a small, one-room repair workshop has grown into a premier showroom and the region's most trusted school rental provider, supporting educators and inspiring musicians of all generations.

APPROACH

Teacher-Approved & Artist-Preferred

We believe that the right instrument is key to unlocking creative potential. That's why we maintain a state-of-the-art luthier and setup lab, where master technicians check, play-test, and calibrate every piece of inventory before it goes home.

By working closely with school band directors and professional performers, we ensure our selection meets strict pedagogical standards, offering players a seamless playing experience from day one.

TIMELINE

Milestones

1995

The Repair Shop

Arthur Pendelton opens a small, one-room workshop in downtown Los Angeles, building a reputation for meticulous guitar setups and string adjustments.

2005

Beverly Hills Flagship

Allegro expands into its flagship Wilshire Blvd showroom, introducing a hand-selected collection of premium grand pianos and designer acoustic guitars.

2015

School Band Programs

Launches the student rental division. By partnering with local school districts, we supply top-tier sanitized band instruments to over 10,000 students.
